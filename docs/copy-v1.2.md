### Principle

*Note:* The differentiator: rigorous evaluation is Eva's research field. It echoes Gregor's "avoiding hype, delivering measurable value".

- **EN**: **Measured, not promised.** Many AI projects are judged by a demo. We judge ours by a comparison: the new model against your current method, on your data, with a fair score agreed up front. If it does not beat what you have, we tell you — before you invest in running it. Rigorous evaluation is not an add-on for us. It is Eva’s research field.
- **DE**: **Gemessen, nicht versprochen.** Viele KI-Projekte werden an einer Demo gemessen. Wir messen unsere an einem Vergleich: das neue Modell gegen Ihre bisherige Methode, auf Ihren Daten, mit einem fairen Maßstab, den wir vorher festlegen. Schlägt es Ihre Lösung nicht, sagen wir es Ihnen – bevor Sie in den Betrieb investieren. Saubere Evaluation ist für uns kein Zusatz. Sie ist Evas Forschungsgebiet.

### Work

*Note:* Proof. Real client work beats papers for a business buyer. The fourth slot is a placeholder until a client project can be named.

- **EN — Selected work**
  - *Forecasting · joint project · 2024* — **Rainfall forecasts for northern tropical Africa**: With colleagues at KIT and HITS, we combined a deep-learning image model with a statistical method for uncertainty to forecast 24-hour rainfall. It beat the physics-based ensemble of the European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts (ECMWF), both on whether it rains and on how much. ([link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.03746))
  - *Computer vision · Gregor · DKFZ* — **nnU-Net: medical image segmentation that configures itself**: Gregor is a co-author of the original nnU-Net paper: a framework that adapts itself to a new segmentation task without manual tuning, and has become a widely used baseline in medical imaging. ([link](https://arxiv.org/abs/1809.10486))
  - *Evaluation · Eva · 2026* — **Fair comparisons of AI and physics-based weather models**: Eva co-developed the potential CRPS, a measure that lets AI weather models and classical ones be compared on equal terms. Published in Artificial Intelligence for the Earth Systems. ([link](https://journals.ametsoc.org/view/journals/aies/5/1/AIES-D-25-0054.1.xml))

### How we work

*Note:* Proposal, not agreed. It gives an email writer a picture of what happens next. Delete it if you'd rather keep the page shorter.

- **EN — How we work**
  1. **You write to us**: Tell us what you want to predict or recognise, and what data you have. A few sentences are enough.
  2. **We check feasibility**: We look at the problem and the data and tell you honestly whether AI will pay off, and what a fair test would look like.
  3. **We build, measure, hand over**: The model, its comparison against your current method, deployment, and a team that can run it.
- **DE — So arbeiten wir**
  1. **Sie schreiben uns**: Beschreiben Sie, was Sie vorhersagen oder erkennen möchten und welche Daten Sie haben. Ein paar Sätze reichen.
  2. **Wir prüfen die Machbarkeit**: Wir sehen uns Aufgabe und Daten an und sagen Ihnen ehrlich, ob sich KI lohnt – und wie ein fairer Test aussähe.
  3. **Wir bauen, messen, übergeben**: Das Modell, sein Vergleich mit Ihrer bisherigen Methode, die Inbetriebnahme – und ein Team, das es weiterführen kann.
